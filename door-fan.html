The Project

My room runs 80°F on Texas summer days because three of four walls back against an attic — and Texas attics get brutal. I built a fan inside my cat door that creates lower pressure in the room, drawing cooled air from the hallway vent rather than letting it return to the ceiling.

The key engineering detail: the motor shaft runs through the hinge's pivot axis. This means the cat door can still swing open and closed while the fan keeps running — the motor stays powered regardless of door position. This was one of my first electronics projects, and it taught me motor control basics — and the painful importance of bearings (I initially had none, and the friction destroyed consistency).
