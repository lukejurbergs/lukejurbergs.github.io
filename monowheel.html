The Idea

One summer day, I bent a LEGO tread piece in reverse and had an idea: build a monowheel. A monowheel has exactly one ground contact point — the wheel — and must somehow still turn and balance. Even modern full-size monowheels fail this at slow speeds, requiring high velocity for gyroscopic stability and the driver boots on the ground for turning. Mine had to work indoors, at slow speeds, using LEGO motors.

After 10 LEGO prototypes that all kept falling over, I realized the project had exceeded what LEGO allowed. So I opened OnShape for the first time and designed a solution from scratch. This was the project that made me know I wanted to be an engineer.

Key Innovations

GYR
Custom 3D-Printed Gyroscope

Designed with inserts for washers to concentrate mass at the rim — maximizing rotational inertia without scaling up the wheel diameter. Provides enough gyroscopic stability for reliable operation at slow indoor speeds without a running start.

STR
Gear Assembly with Rim Friction Pads

The core problem: how do you exert directional force from inside a moving wheel onto its exterior? The solution: a gear assembly with friction pads pressing against the inside of the rim — allowing steering torque to transfer to the wheel without a second ground contact. The custom frame has precision slots for the gear train that mesh with the LEGO design grid.

What I Learned

01
CAD as a thinking tool, not just a drawing tool. OnShape forced me to solve the mechanical problem completely before printing anything. That shift — from build-and-iterate to think-then-model — made me a better engineer across every project that followed.
02
Managing a project independently. No team, no class structure, no deadlines. Just a problem I wanted to solve and twelve versions to get there. That's the purest form of engineering motivation, and I've chased it in every project since.
03
Constraints clarify. No adhesives, max 4 motors, 3D prints under 250mm. Every constraint forced a more elegant, repairable, rethought solution than I would have found with unlimited options.
