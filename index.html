THE
ENGINEER
BEHIND
THE WORK
I'm Luke, a high school student at LASA in Austin, TX. Mechanical Engineering is my strongest skillset, but I've pushed beyond it into PCB design, Python, C++, electronics, art, and film.

My goal is not only to be a mechanical engineer but also a leader. I learned what leadership actually looks like when I directed my short film, Paranoid. I didn't just hand out roles and deadlines. I acted in it. I co-edited it. I helped build the sets. I knew what it felt like to be the editor staring at a timeline at 3 a.m. the night before upload day, because I was there too. I knew how hard it was to get your lines right under pressure, because I was doing it alongside my team.

That's the kind of engineer I want to be. Someone who can work shoulder to shoulder with the electrical engineer, the coder, and the designer, and actually understand what they're up against. The best work I've been part of didn't come from out-of-touch leaders making demands. It came from a group of people building together, with someone at the center who knew when to lead, and when to get in the trenches.
