A/C CAT HOUSE

Woodworking · August 2023

CategoryEngineering
TimelineWoodworking · August 2023

The Project

My neighbor's cat, Lava, was sometimes left outside during long Austin summer days. Rather than letting him into our house (where he might get lost), I built him a shaded, ventilated shelter from materials we already had: scrap wood, some tubing, an old cat bed, and a fan.

The fan pulls air through a duct from inside the house into the shelter, keeping the interior cooler than the outdoor ambient temperature. This was my first independent woodworking project — no instructions, just a problem, a goal, and scrap materials. It worked. Lava used it.
