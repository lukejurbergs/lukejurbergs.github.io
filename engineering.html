ENGINEER
ING

These aren't just projects — they're a record of how I think. Every one of these started with a constraint I didn't know how to solve, and ended with me understanding something I couldn't have learned any other way.

Science Olympiad pushed me hardest. The hovercraft and electric vehicle took 6-month sprints of daily work, repeated failure, and late-night rebuilds. The smaller projects — a pit bike with no magneto, a monowheel that kept falling over — taught me just as much about persistence.

HOVERCRAFT

A hovering vehicle that traverses a 185cm track in a precise time window — with no microchips, no springs, and no rheostat. The final design used a cam-based mechanical timer as the control system, treating the motor itself as the clock.
